Month one is dedicated to designing the next twelve: scripts, campaign concepts, visual references, prompt libraries, narrative systems, production documentation, and every creative foundation required to execute the year's storytelling.

Once the foundation is set, the following twelve months run as a single continuous production cycle — no gaps, no pauses. A non-stopping stream of campaigns, assets, and storytelling built on top of the system designed in month one.

production system

5 formats.

Every campaign is delivered through one of five production formats — distributed strategically across your annual communication system.

ai brand films 4 / year
multiple shots / immersive storytelling

The most ambitious expression of your brand. A cinematic production designed to communicate vision, emotion, and identity through stories that define how your company is remembered.

ai shorts 4 / month
4 shots / compact storytelling

A condensed cinematic experience, designed to communicate meaningful stories through concise, carefully crafted cinematic language.

ai clips 4 / month
editorial standard · 10 seconds · 2 shots / one idea

The shortest format in the system. Not every story needs a beginning and an end — sometimes a single moment is enough to define an entire campaign.

ai carousels 2 / month
the editorial-standard photo essay

Ideas transformed into memorable visuals. Carousels combine art direction, typography, and composition to communicate concepts that photography alone cannot express.

ai graphics 1 / month
visual stories / artboards / editorial posters

Ideas transformed into memorable visuals — concepts that photography alone cannot express, combining art direction, typography, and composition into a single striking image.

good questions

before you reach out.

Is AI-generated content actually good enough for a premium brand?

That's the entire premise of pssycco: we don't produce content, we build premium brand assets designed to reinforce your company's market authority through exceptional storytelling, cinematic execution, and enduring brand perception. If your brand also needs traditional, non-AI production, the Signature membership blends both under one creative direction.

How fast do we start seeing content?

Month one — Ensemble — is dedicated entirely to building your year: scripts, concepts, visual references, prompt libraries, and narrative systems. From month two, production runs as a continuous, non-stopping cycle for the remaining twelve months.

Do I need my own AI production agency?

Only for the Leaders membership, where pssycco handles strategy and creative direction while your existing AI agency executes production. Ultimate and Signature are end-to-end: pssycco directs and produces everything in-house.

What happens with my brand's assets after the engagement?

Every membership includes an annual brand archive and the AI Brand Identity System — a structured, proprietary archive of prompts, narrative frameworks, visual references, and production guidelines, so the work continues guiding future teams and agencies, not just the current campaign.

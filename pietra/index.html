the ultimate membership

ultimate gives you more ways to create.

The Ultimate Membership is built around pssycco's AI production system and does not include on-location non-AI production. However, any existing footage provided by the client can be integrated into the content system at no additional production cost.

For brands looking to expand into original on-location production, pssycco offers a separate production system:

  • brand films$7,000,000 COP
  • signature videos$4,500,000 COP
  • photo studios$700,000 COP

These productions can be added whenever the brand needs them, without changing the structure of the Ultimate Membership.

flexibility

one system. any source.

Any AI-generated content included in the grid — shorts, clips, or clips + photo studios — can be replaced with existing non-AI material provided by the client. This allows the brand to combine AI production with real footage whenever the story benefits from a more dynamic edit, additional shots, or existing brand material.
